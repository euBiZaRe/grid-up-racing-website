🏎️ Race Event Overview

  • • Timed multiclass racing for teams of 1–2 drivers
    ↳ 2 drivers per car are strongly encouraged
    ↳ Solo drivers are allowed if a teammate cannot attend
  • • All 10 GT3 cars and all 6 GT4 cars are eligible
    ↳ Open setups are allowed
  • • All cars will be limited to:
    ↳ 60% fuel capacity
    ↳ 2 additional tire sets per race
    ↳ ~30 minute fuel stints
    ↳ No tire changes at every stop
  • • Fair-share driving rules apply
    ↳ Any registered driver must complete at least one stint
    ↳ Failure to do so will result in disqualification
  • • Each team receives 1 Fast Repair
    ↳ Use it wisely 👀

📋 Registration Guidelines

GT4 CLASS

Teams with one or more drivers under 2,000 iRating should register for GT4.

GT3 CLASS

Teams with both drivers above 2,000 iRating should register for GT3.

⚠️ Standards & Penalties

  • • Clean racing standards apply at all times
    ↳ Before the event, during the race, and after the checkered flag.
  • • In multiclass racing:
    ↳ The car ahead has the right to the racing line, even if it is in a slower class.
    ↳ Slower-class cars are expected to drive predictably.
  • • Passing responsibility:
    ↳ Drivers attempting a pass are responsible for completing it safely.
    ↳ No late-braking divebombs without established overlap.

    • 🚨 Incident Limits

    Race administration reserves the right to issue black flags, disqualify teams, or penalize poor sportsmanship or reckless driving.
