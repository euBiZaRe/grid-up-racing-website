
<p>The Ardennes forest played host to a motorsport spectacle on Saturday as the GRiD UP GT Challenge tackled the Circuit de Spa-Francorchamps. In a grueling 3-hour multiclass battle, drivers had to contend with changing track dynamics, traffic congestion, and highly competitive fields. When the chequered flag finally fell, it was Alexander Cortez who took a dramatic GT3 victory by a mere 0.428 seconds, while Stephen Smalley put on a clinic in the GT4 class to take a commanding win.</p>

<p>Spa-Francorchamps' legendary combination of high-speed sweeps and unforgiving walls meant that survival was just as critical as outright speed. With 62 laps completed in the GT3 class and 56 in GT4, the race saw intense strategizing, heavy tyre degradation, and several hard-fought battles through iconic sections like Eau Rouge, Pouhon, and the Bus Stop chicane.</p>

<h2>GT3: Cortez Defeats Claudio in Grand Stand Finish</h2>

<p>The GT3 class was defined by a tactical war of attrition and a frantic final stint. Jason Holland of Bitepoint Racing started from pole position, but the lead quickly became a shifting target. The race recorded 7 lead changes among 6 different leaders, highlighting the depth of the field.</p>

<p>Alexander Claudio of BWE Racing was the dominant force for much of the mid-race, leading a race-high 30 laps and laying down a blisteringly consistent pace. However, Alexander Cortez mounted a late-race charge. Slicing through traffic and maintaining optimal tyre wear, Cortez closed the gap to Claudio in the closing laps. The final lap saw bumper-to-bumper action, with Cortez holding off Claudio's final attack at the Bus Stop to take the victory by a razor-thin margin of 0.428 seconds.</p>

<p>Harrison Holliday of Bite Point Racing claimed the final spot on the podium. Holliday put in a stellar drive to earn Hard Charger honors, recovering from early setbacks and setting the fastest lap of the race with an incredible 2:16.559. Scott Mowry and pole-sitter Jason Holland rounded out the top five, while Chandler English finished a respectable sixth in his prototype GT3 campaign.</p>

<h2>GT4: Smalley Dominates the Field</h2>

<p>In the GT4 class, Stephen Smalley of Mr Sparkle proved to be in a league of his own. Securing pole position, Smalley dictated the pace from start to finish. Over the course of 56 laps, Smalley managed the gap to the field, avoiding major incidents to take a commanding victory by 13.459 seconds.</p>

<p>Marsalis Simms put in a strong performance to secure second place, surviving intense pressure from the chasing pack. Behind them, Andrew B. Fabian of GRiD UP claimed third. Fabian was the star of the GT4 mid-pack, driving an aggressive race to earn Hard Charger honors and setting the fastest lap of the GT4 class with a 2:29.970.</p>

<p>David Shreve and Adam L. Jones rounded out the top five, while Michael Graham finished sixth despite a challenging race that saw him collect 39 incidents. A.J. Johnson took seventh, finishing four laps down.</p>
