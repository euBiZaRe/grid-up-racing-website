Official Regulations

Championship Rules

BMW M2 Racing Cup Season 1 — Sporting & Technical Regulations

01 Championship Format

Season 1 consists of 10 rounds, each a 60-minute endurance session on iRacing using the BMW M2 CS Racing car on fixed setups. Up to 35 grid slots are available.

  • Cars are spec — no setup changes permitted
  • Races held fortnightly on Saturday evenings (UK time)
  • iRacing practice lap times determine starting grid
  • Season finale: Brands Hatch Grand Prix circuit
02 Driver Classification

Two classes compete simultaneously for separate championships:

  • Pro Class — 2500 iRating or above at time of registration
  • AM Class — below 2500 iRating at time of registration

Class is locked at registration. Disputes must be raised with the Race Director before Round 1.

03 Points System

Points awarded per class separately. +1 bonus point for the overall fastest lap.

Drop Round: Each driver's single worst-scoring round is removed from their total to produce their Net Points. Final positions are determined by Net Points.

04 Conduct & Sportsmanship
  • Deliberate contact, pit manoeuvres, and intentional blocking are prohibited
  • Drivers must leave a car's width when a competitor is alongside
  • Corner-cutting that gains a lasting advantage must be corrected immediately
  • Unsportsmanlike behaviour in post-race chat will be penalised
  • Incidents exceeding 4x on iRacing may trigger sporting review
05 Protests & Stewarding
  • Protests must be filed within 24 hours of the race result being published
  • Video evidence is strongly recommended
  • Steward decisions are final and binding
  • Penalties include: time additions, points deductions, grid drops, or race bans
  • Frivolous protests may incur a sporting penalty
06 Car Numbers

Each driver must hold a unique car number between #1 and #999. Numbers are claimed first-come, first-served at registration and held for the full season.

  • Numbers cannot be transferred between drivers
  • Vacated numbers return to the pool after 7 days
