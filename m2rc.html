Sporting Regulations

BMW M2 Racing Cup official rules, points system, and conduct policies for Season 4.

1. Championship Format

Season 4 consists of 10 rounds, each a 60-minute endurance session on iRacing using the official BMW M2 CS Racing car on fixed setups to ensure the closest possible racing. Up to 35 grid slots are available. Cars are spec — no setup changes permitted.

2. Points System & Drop Rounds

Points are awarded to the top 20 finishers of each round:

🥇 1st: 25pts  |  🥈 2nd: 20pts  |  🥉 3rd: 16pts  |  4th: 13pts  |  5th: 11pts
6th: 10pts  |  7th: 9pts  |  8th: 8pts  |  9th: 7pts  |  10th–20th: decreasing
⚡ Fastest Lap: +1pt  |  🏁 Pole Position: +1pt

Drop Round Rule: Each driver's single lowest-scoring round is automatically excluded from their total at season end — accounting for real-life obligations.

3. Incidents & Penalties

Standard iRacing incident point rules apply. Penalty thresholds:

12x Drive-through penalty applied automatically by iRacing.
17x Disqualification from the session.
Review Protests filed within 24 hours of race completion. Steward panel decisions are final and binding.
