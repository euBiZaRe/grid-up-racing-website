Welcome to the M2CS Cup

The BMW M2 Racing Cup (M2RC) is GRiD UP's premier touring car championship. Running the agile, aggressive BMW M2 CS Racing car, this series brings together tight packs, intense door-to-door battles, and a friendly yet highly competitive esports racing experience.

Whether you are fighting for the Overall Championship, the Rookie Class trophy, or the Team Shield, every point matters. We feature professional commentators, dynamic drop-round calculations, and a dedicated steward panel to guarantee fair competition.

Sporting Regulations

BMW M2 Racing Cup Rules & Policy Guidelines.

1. Championship Format

The BMW M2 CS Cup consists of 6 rounds. All drivers run the official BMW M2 CS Racing car on fixed setups to ensure the closest racing format. Up to 35 grid slots are available.

2. Point System & Drop Rounds

Points are awarded to the top 20 finishers of each round as follows:

1st: 25pts | 2nd: 20pts | 3rd: 16pts | 4th: 13pts | 5th: 11pts | 6th: 10pts | 7th: 9pts | 8th: 8pts...
Fastest Lap: +1pt | Pole Position: +1pt

Drop Round Rule: To account for real-life obligations, each driver's single worst round score (the round with the lowest points earned) is automatically subtracted from their total at the end of the season.

3. Incidents & Penalties

Standard iRacing incident point rules apply. Incident point caps and corresponding penalties are:

  • 12 Incident Points (12x): Drive-through penalty.
  • 17 Incident Points (17x): Disqualification from the session.
  • Steward Review: Protests filed within 24 hours of race completion will be reviewed. Panel decisions are final.
