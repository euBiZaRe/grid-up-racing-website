Event Overview

The iRacing Portimao 1000 is a multi-class, team-based endurance "Special Event" on the iRacing platform that simulates a 1000-kilometer race. It pays tribute to the real-world 1000 Kilometres of Algarve endurance races originally held in 2009 and 2010 at Portugal's Algarve International Circuit.

Cars Competing

LMP2 Class: HPD ARX-01c (Prototype)
GT1 Class: Aston Martin DBR9 & Chevrolet Corvette C6.R
GT2 Class: Ford GT

Algarve International Circuit

Often referred to as Portim�o, the track is highly regarded for severe elevation changes, blind corners, and old-school rhythm.

Rules & Team Dynamics

Vintage Ruleset

Format: Classic 2000s-era ruleset with driver swaps across three vintage machinery categories.

Traffic Management: Crucial to manage speed differences between prototypes (LMP2) and vintage GT classes (GT1/GT2).
