Southern 500

September 2-7, 2026

← Back to Home

Event Overview

The iRacing Southern 500 is a highly anticipated, full-length sim racing event that mirrors the real-world NASCAR "Crown Jewel" race. Held on the famously asymmetrical, egg-shaped Darlington Raceway in South Carolina, this grueling 500-mile special event challenges competitors to manage tire wear and master the notoriously difficult track over roughly 367 laps.

Cars Competing

NASCAR Next Gen Cup Cars: Chevrolet Camaro, Ford Mustang, and Toyota Camry

Track Profile

Darlington Raceway

Known as "The Track Too Tough to Tame". The asymmetrical corners require vastly different car setups at each end, forcing constant handling compromises.

Features: Close, unforgiving walls leading to virtual "Darlington Stripes".

Race Rules & Strategy

Event Details

Length: 367 Laps (500 Miles) requiring careful fuel and tire management over several hours.

Series: Part of the official NASCAR iRacing Series, running parallel to the real-world schedule.

Paint Schemes: Teams frequently utilize custom, vintage-style "throwback" paint schemes to honor NASCAR history.

Race Format

  • Type: Solo Stock Car (Endurance)
  • Distance: 500 Miles (367 Laps)
  • Classes: NASCAR Next Gen Cup
