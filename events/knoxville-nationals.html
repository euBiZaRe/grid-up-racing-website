Event Overview

The iRacing Knoxville Nationals is a premier, week-long iRacing Special Event that simulates the real-life NOS Energy Drink Knoxville Nationals held annually in Iowa. It tasks sim racers with taming the high-speed Dirt 410 Winged Sprint Car at the legendary "Sprint Car Capital of the World".

Cars Competing

Dirt 410 Winged Sprint Car: High-horsepower dirt sprint cars

Track Profile

Knoxville Raceway

The semi-banked dirt oval located in Knoxville, Iowa, widely known as the "Sprint Car Capital of the World".

Format: Daily heats leading to Saturday feature events.

Event Format & Progressions

Knockout Format

Qualifying Heats: Drivers race Monday through Friday to accumulate championship points.

Super Sessions: Saturday features the top 60-70 drivers in weekly points advancing to the top-split Feature.

Alphabet Soup: Exciting ladder format (E, D, C, and B Mains) where drivers transfer up to reach the coveted 22-car, 35-lap A-Main.

Race Format

  • Type: Solo Dirt Oval
  • Distance: 35-Lap A-Main (Heats Match)
  • Classes: 410 Winged Sprint Car
