Brickyard 400

July 22-27, 2026

← Back to Home

Event Overview

The iRacing Brickyard 400 is a highly anticipated annual virtual endurance event that replicates NASCAR's real-world "crown jewel" race at the historic Indianapolis Motor Speedway 2.5-mile oval. It is a massive 160-lap (400-mile) competition where sim racers drive current-generation NASCAR Cup Series cars to battle for a spot in the winner's circle.

Cars Competing

NASCAR Next Gen Cup Cars: Chevrolet Camaro, Ford Mustang, and Toyota Camry

Track Profile

Indianapolis Motor Speedway

The iconic 2.5-mile oval in Speedway, Indiana, demanding precise throttle control and drafting strategy.

Distance: 160 Laps (400 Miles)

Series & Significance

NASCAR iRacing Series

This special event is part of the broader NASCAR iRacing Series, running parallel to the real-life motorsports calendar.

Status: Widely considered one of the platform's major "crown jewel" oval races.

Race Format

  • Type: Solo Stock Car
  • Distance: 400 Miles (160 Laps)
  • Classes: NASCAR Next Gen Cup
