Event Overview

The iRacing Suzuka 1000km is a premier, team-based endurance racing event on the platform's official Special Events calendar. It challenges drivers to complete 173 laps (or roughly 1,000 kilometers) around Japan�s iconic, highly technical Suzuka Circuit.

Cars Competing

GT3 Class: Typically features GT3 cars (and sometimes GT4 or Cup cars depending on iteration).

Track Profile

Suzuka Circuit

Japan's iconic, highly technical figure-eight layout featuring the famous S-Curves, Spoon Curve, and high-speed 130R.

Distance: 173 Laps (1000 Kilometers)

Team Rules & Scheduling

Event Details

Team Requirement: Solo racing is not permitted. Mandatory team format requiring at least two drivers.

Duration: Typically takes around 6 to 6.5 hours to complete.

Schedule: Multiple weekend time slots (Friday through Sunday) to accommodate global time zones.

Race Format

  • Type: Team Endurance
  • Distance: 1000 Kilometers (~6H)
  • Classes: GT3 Class
