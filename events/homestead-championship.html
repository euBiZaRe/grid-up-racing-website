Homestead Championship

November 4-9, 2026

← Back to Home

Event Overview

The iRacing Homestead Championship is the thrilling, full-length, 36-week NASCAR iRacing Series (NiS) season finale held at the virtual Homestead-Miami Speedway. Mirroring the real-world NASCAR schedule, it acts as the climactic final race to crown the champion of the official NASCAR iRacing Series.

Cars Competing

NASCAR Cup Series Cars: Current-generation NASCAR Cup machinery

Track Profile

Homestead-Miami Speedway

The famous 1.5-mile variable-banked oval. Features progressive banking up to 20 degrees near the wall and is heavily demanding on tire preservation.

Format: Full-length championship race.

Series & Legacy

NASCAR iRacing Series

The championship spans 36 events matching the real NASCAR Cup Series calendar, letting drivers earn points over months of racing.

Historical Value: Serving as the final stage of 4 high-stakes events in the championship phase, it regularly serves as the championship decider.

Race Format

  • Type: Solo Stock Car (Championship)
  • Distance: Full Cup Length
  • Classes: NASCAR Cup Series
