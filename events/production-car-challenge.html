Event Overview

The iRacing Production Car Challenge (officially the Sim-Lab Production Car Challenge) is a popular, short-duration, multi-class racing series. It is specifically designed to teach beginner and intermediate drivers essential skills like traffic management, spatial awareness, and racecraft in a fun, accessible environment.

Cars Competing

BMW M4 GT4: The fastest class, navigating traffic.
Toyota GR86: High mid-corner speed sports coupe.
Renault Clio: Front-wheel-drive hot hatch requiring unique entry technique.
Global Mazda MX-5 Cup: Nimble momentum starter class.

Track Profile

Global Road Courses

Races are hosted at various historic road courses worldwide, prioritizing passing technique and spatial awareness.

Duration: Short format 25 to 30 minute sprint races.

Multiclass Etiquette

Traffic Management

Dynamics: MX-5, GR86, and Clio have very close lap times, leading to tight pack racing.

Etiquette: Faster cars (GT4) must pass cleanly, and slower classes must hold predictable lines.

Race Format

  • Type: Solo Sprint (Multiclass)
  • Distance: 25-30 Minute Races
  • Classes: GT4, GR86, Clio, MX-5
