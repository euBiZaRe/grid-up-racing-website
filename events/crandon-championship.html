Crandon Championship

August 25-30, 2026

← Back to Home

Event Overview

The iRacing Crandon Championship is an annual, week-long Special Event featuring high-horsepower Pro 4 off-road trucks at the real-life Crandon International Raceway. It is iRacing's only major official dirt road special event.

Cars Competing

Off-Road Pro 4 Truck: Heavy 4-wheel-drive machine boasting nearly 900 horsepower with sequential transmission and no driving aids.

Track Profile

Crandon International Raceway

Known as the "Big House" of short course off-road racing in Wisconsin, featuring massive dirt jumps, sharp hairpins, and the famous high-speed "Land Rush" start.

Start Type: Land Rush side-by-side launch.

Event Format & Requirements

Event Details

Structure: Runs throughout the week, leading up to intense weekend "Super Sessions".

Qualification: Preliminary point races determine division for the final weekend splits.

License: Dirt Road license of D with a 4.0 Safety Rating or higher.

Race Format

  • Type: Solo Dirt Road
  • Distance: Sprint Format
  • Classes: Pro 4 Off-Road Truck
