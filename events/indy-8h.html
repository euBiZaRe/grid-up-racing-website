Indy 8 Hour

October 16-18, 2026

← Back to Home

Event Overview

In the world of sim racing, the iRacing Indy 8H (or Indy 8 Hour) is a premier GT3 sports car endurance event that mirrors the real-world Indianapolis Motor Speedway event. It challenges teams of two or more drivers to race around the iconic IMS road course for 8 hours.

Cars Competing

GT3 Class: Replicates the models running in the real-world Intercontinental GT Challenge

Track Profile

Indianapolis Road Course

The challenging road course layout utilizing parts of the famous brickyard oval, combining high-speed sections with slow, technical corners.

Duration: Exactly 8 Hours of continuous racing

Driver Requirements & Splits

Event Details

Real-world Link: Virtual counterpart to the Intercontinental GT Challenge (IGTC) and GT World Challenge America finale.

License: Minimum C-class sports car license to ensure safe and clean racing.

Splits: Teams are assigned to splits based on their combined average sports car iRating.

Race Format

  • Type: Team Endurance
  • Distance: 8 Hours
  • Classes: GT3 Class
