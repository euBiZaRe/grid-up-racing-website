Petit Le Mans

September 25-27, 2026

← Back to Home

Event Overview

The iRacing Petit Le Mans is a premier, annual endurance sim-racing event that mirrors the real-world 10-hour sports car race held at Michelin Raceway Road Atlanta. As part of iRacing’s official Special Events calendar, it challenges teams of drivers to share a single car in multiclass racing.

Cars Competing

Multiclass Field: GTP, LMP2 prototypes and GT3/GTD cars competing simultaneously

Track Profile

Road Atlanta

Located in Braselton, Georgia—a famous, twisting 2.54-mile circuit renowned for its dramatic elevation changes and fast-flowing corners.

Duration: Exactly 10 Hours continuous racing

Requirements & Team Rules

Endurance Rules

Team size: Multiple drivers swapping in and out of the same virtual car.

License: Requires team registration and proper sportscar license rating to ensure clean traffic management.

Race Format

  • Type: Team Endurance (Multiclass)
  • Distance: 10 Hours
  • Classes: GTP, LMP2, GT3/GTD
