Event Overview

The iRacing SCCA Runoffs is a premier annual special event on the platform that digitally replicates the Sports Car Club of America (SCCA) National Championship. It brings together virtual versions of iconic amateur racing cars for a competitive, week-long championship showdown.

Cars Competing

Amateur Sports Cars: Formula Vee, Spec Racer Ford, Mazda MX-5, USF 2000, GT4 Class (covers 6-8 diverse classes)

Track Profile

Road America

Races are generally hosted at historic, real-life SCCA tracks such as Road America in Elkhart Lake, Wisconsin, testing technical precision across complex configurations.

Format: Week-long point accumulation leading to Saturday finals.

Race Format & Grid Seeding

Super Session Format

Grid Seeding: Saturday's main feature sessions are split based on championship points earned during the week (rather than just iRating).

Races: Typically features hour-long blocks containing 35-minute individual timed races for different vehicle classes.

Race Format

  • Type: Solo Sprint (Multiclass Blocks)
  • Distance: 35-Minute Timed Races
  • Classes: Formula Vee, SRF, MX-5, GT4
